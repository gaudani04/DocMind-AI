Raj Shamani

Born	29 July 1997 (age 28)[1]
Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India
Occupations	podcaster, entrepreneur
Known for	Figuring Out with Raj Shamani
Raj Shamani (born 29 July 1997)[2] is an Indian entrepreneur, podcaster, and author. He is primarily known for hosting the business podcast Figuring Out with Raj Shamani, which became the top-ranked podcast in India on Spotify in 2025.[3] Shamani began his career in his family's FMCG business[4] before transitioning into digital content creation and public speaking. He has represented India at the United Nations Youth Assembly and is the author of the self-help book Build, Don't Talk.[5]

Early life and education
Shamani was born into a Marwari family in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. His father, Suresh Shamani, was a businessman involved in textile and chemical trading[6]. At the age of 16, following his father's health issues and the family's financial distress, Shamani took an active role in the family business.[4] He attended the Emerald Heights International School and later Prestige Institute of Management in Indore, though he eventually focused on his entrepreneurial ventures over formal higher education.[citation needed]

Business Career
Shamani Industries
In 2013, Shamani launched a dishwashing liquid brand called "Jadugar Drop" with a seed capital of ₹10,000 borrowed from his father. He initially sold the product door-to-door in Indore.[4] By 2015, he merged his venture with his father's chemical trading company to form Shamani Industries. As of 2020, the company reportedly reached a turnover of ₹200 crore, producing various household cleaning agents.[7]

House of X and Investing
Shamani co-founded "House of X," a platform designed to help content creators launch their own consumer brands. Additionally, he is an angel investor in several Indian startups, including Classplus, GrowthSchool, and Wint Wealth.[8][7]

Content Creation and Media
Shamani began his career in digital content creation with motivational videos on Instagram and YouTube. In 2021, he launched his podcast, Figuring Out..., focused on personal growth and discussions with notable guests.[8]

Shamani has participated in multiple speaking competitions and debates. He represented India at the United Nations, and addressed Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the National Creators Award in 2024.[8] In October 2025, Shamani addressed the Sharjah Investment Forum–World Investment Conference (SIF-WIC) in Sharjah, where he discussed the concept of "emotional ROI", arguing that trust and purpose can function as forms of capital for creators in the digital economy.[9]

In June 2025, Vijay Mallya appeared in public for the first time in nine years on Shamani's podcast. The interview of 4.3 hours went viral and received 20 million views in four days.[10][11][12][13]

Other notable guests who have featured on Shamani's podcast include Emmanuel Macron Bill Gates, Jackson Wang, Riri Trivedi[14] and Karan Johar.[15][16][17]
